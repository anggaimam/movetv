# T-11: TV measurements (fill in and send photos)

Turn the overlay on: hold ◀ for 2 s (or press P on a keyboard). Photograph the TV after about 30 seconds of play.

The overlay now shows three lines:
1. `fps  worst ms  AI fps  HIGH/LOW`
2. `scene  upd avg/max  draw avg/max ms  heap MB  native MB` (CPU time only; if draw is small but fps is low, the GPU is the limit)
3. `lock ...`

Targets: menus ≥ 55 fps, games ≥ 50 fps with 1 player, ≥ 40 fps with 2 players, AI fps within 1 of idle (12–14 for 1P, about 7 for 2P).
